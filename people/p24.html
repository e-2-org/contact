With the help of Paul Danvers, the art therapist, all participants of We all peel the onions were invited to a discussion with me in the art room at the Day Hospital (part of the Royal Free NHS Trust). A small number of participants attended and the group members fluctuated throughout the afternoon, typical of the drop-in nature of Wednesday art sessions at the Day Hospital. This followed the formal evaluations carried out by Camden Arts Centre in consultation with the participants and was intended purely as an opportunity for their voices to appear on Contact. We all peel the onions raised many important issues and problems for all involved and the following extracts represent only several perspectives. (I was asked by Camden Arts Centre to omit various comments). One participant wished to be named, the others preferred to be represented anonymously.
