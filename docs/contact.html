As you stroll through Contact you will meet fragments of the North London Link projects and its audience. These encounters are a condensed selection of voices from a research based on conversations.

From street corners to street parties, from car parks to cricket grounds, as a stranger handing out gifts, I captured and encouraged interpretations towards the art works out in the field. I have arranged and rearranged, cut and cut and these selected impressions now fall within seven themes which grew from my findings.

In any direction, you can read a dialogue between people who never met, revealing some of the thoughts, dreams, concerns, frustrations and imaginations that were and are the North London Link experience.
