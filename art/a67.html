We all peel the onions Christine and Irene Hohenbüchler
with participants from a Day Hospital
linked to the Royal Free Hospital NHS Trust

Camden Arts Centre, London NW3
4 February - 26 March 2000

From November through December 1999, Christine and Irene Hohenbüchler made collaborative works of art with a group of psychiatric patients at a Day Hospital linked to the Royal Free Hospital NHS Trust. They developed projects and activities that challenged and explored expectations and approaches to art and therapy, by choosing unusual materials and asking participants to produce images relating to their personal experiences. The results of these collaborations were exhibited by the Hohenbüchler sisters at Camden Arts Centre. The debate and analysis that accompanied the project resulted in a special edition of the publication Victorya, curated by Grant Watson.

The Hohenbüchler sisters have been working both together and independently for ten years, and their practice regularly extends to collaboration on multiple authorship projects, working with people living in or attending medical treatment centres (particularly in the area of mental health), educational groups or penal institutions. Recent exhibitions include Documenta X, Kassel 1997 and the Venice Biennale 1999.

The project was possible thanks to the assistance of Anthony Auerbach at the Austrian Cultural Institute, Grant Watson, Paul Danvers, Kay Thompson and the Royal Free NHS Trust.
