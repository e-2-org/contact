Better Scenery Adam Chodzko

Car park and lower lobby, O2 Centre, Finchley Road, London NW3
17 December 1999 - 27 August 2000

Better Scenery was an escapist proposition which connected the Arizona Desert, USA, and Finchley Road, London. In each location, a sign gave written directions to the other place. Together they suggested the possibility of travelling from the Painted Desert, in the middle of nowhere, to the urban car park of the O2 leisure centre and vice-versa. Photographs of both signs in situ were displayed in the lower lobby of the O2. Though thousands of miles apart, the signs signified an imaginary space, reflected in the backdrop of imitation rocks, water features and tropical sounds of the O2 leisure centre.

Adam Chodzko's installation continued his exploration of transitional states. He has previously conducted searches for people who think they look like God within the pages of Loot newspaper, and the 'murdered' adolescents in Pasolini's film Salu; and asked lighting technicians to stage their version of the appearance of heaven. Chodzko has exhibited widely in Britain and internationally, including the Ikon Gallery, Birmingham 1999, in Dream Machines at Camden Arts Centre and touring, 2000, and the British Academy, Rome 2000.
