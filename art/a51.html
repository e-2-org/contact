On Finchley Road Maurice O'Connell

Midland Crescent, Finchley Road, London NW3
15-17 September 2000

In consultation and conversation with local businesses, shopkeepers and communities, Maurice O'Connell began researching possibilities for change on the Finchley Road during his residency at Camden Arts Centre in April 2000. O'Connell proposed interventions in various locations to highlight the history and development of the Finchley Road. The project itself was shaped by the artist's method of consultation with local people. These dialogues focused on Midland Crescent, a faded, mysterious gateway leading away from the incessant traffic of Finchley Road, where a gala celebration took place over a weekend, highlighting the area with bunting, strings of lights and newly painted walls. Passers by were invited to join the celebration and asked to reveal their attitudes about and ambitions for the area. The project is a starting point for future consultation and collaborative work between specific communities along Finchley Road and Camden Arts Centre.

Maurice O'Connell's work has been presented in museums, galleries and a wide range of non-gallery spaces in Ireland, the UK, Europe, South America and the USA. Notable projects include Conversations at the Castle, Atlanta, 1995; The Handpainted O, CCA, Glasgow, 1996; Tranzit, a major public art programme in Ventspil, Latvia in 1998 and in the same year, an audience development project for the Sao Paulo Biennale.
