Journeys Esther Sayers, Anthony O'Flaherty, Claudia Kappenberg
with members of the Deaf community

Swiss Cottage Central Library, London NW6
29 November 1999 - 30 January 2000

Journeys is a video work which follows eight individuals on their travels - real and imaginary. A collaborative project, this travelogue began as a series of practical workshops with members of the deaf community. Working together with the artists, each participant filmed and edited their own individual journey. Two commentaries act as guides; one signed by each participant in British Sign Language and another spoken. Journeys presents new ideas about conditions of access for deaf or hard of hearing people, placing them at the centre of their own experience and giving them the choice of what to communicate to a hearing audience.

Esther Sayers, Anthony O'Flaherty and Claudia Kappenberg work extensively both as artists and within art education, and are all based in London. Their individual practices include video, printmaking, photography and glasswork.

Journeys was funded by the Arts Council of England Artists in Sites for Learning Scheme and Camden Libraries and Information Services.
