Untitled (America) 1994/95 Felix Gonzalez-Torres

Lymington Road, London NW3
19 May - 16 July 2000

Untitled (America) 1994/95 comprised garlands of lights installed across a street opposite the Centre. Like all Gonzalez-Torres' work, the piece is on one level concerned with the intersecting dialogues between the realms of public and private, social and personal. Its intention is also as a decorative and celebratory piece. Gonzalez-Torres died in 1996 and a large exhibition of his work took place at the Serpentine Gallery, with other satellite projects through the duration of this project.

Gonzalez-Torres exhibited widely in America and Europe and showed alongside Ad Reinhardt and Joseph Kosuth in Symptoms of Interference, Conditions of Possibility at Camden Arts Centre in 1994.

Organised by Camden Arts Centre in partnership with the Serpentine Gallery. Supported by Edwin Cohen and Andrea Rosen.
